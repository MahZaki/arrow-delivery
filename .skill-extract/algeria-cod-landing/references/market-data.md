## Customer Pain Points (Algerian E-commerce Merchants)

1. **Lost/stolen packages** — trust in courier is paramount; insurance and guarantees matter
2. **Cash float delay** — merchants want COD funds transferred quickly (daily/weekly)
3. **Return management** — high return rates in Algeria; need clear exchange/return process
4. **Poor communication** — merchants hate not knowing where packages are; tracking is critical
5. **Rural/southern coverage** — reaching Sahara wilayas is a differentiator
6. **Friday delivery** — Algeria has a Thursday/Friday weekend; Sunday–Thursday business week
7. **Instagram/Facebook shop integration** — most Algerian e-commerce is social-commerce

## Conversion Triggers for Algerian Audience

1. **Wilaya count badge** (prominent number) — "58 wilayas" = maximum credibility
2. **COD guarantee** — "Recouvrement sécurisé" builds merchant trust
3. **Free pickup offer** — "Ramassage gratuit dès 10 colis" removes friction
4. **WhatsApp CTA** — preferred over email for B2B contact in Algeria
5. **Algerian flag / local identity** — signals "we understand your market"
6. **Years in business** — "Depuis 2013" builds credibility in a market with many new entrants
7. **Known client logos** — Jumia, SoqDZ, local brand logos boost trust
8. **Certification badges** — ISO certs from Yalidine have raised the bar; others follow
9. **24h promise** — speed is the #1 competitive claim
10. **App availability** — Huawei AppGallery badge matters (many Algerians use Huawei phones)
